Product

MythOS98 — The Operating System for Life Itself

One simulated layer for work, leisure, logistics, and everything in between. MythOS98 is Monogon's most complete release yet — built to be the only environment you ever need to log into.

Core Features

💼Simulated Workspaces

Full office environments rendered on demand. Work from home without ever technically leaving the office — commute times drop to zero, load times become the new traffic.

👑FantasyLand Integration

Drop straight from your workspace into a fully themed leisure instance. Medieval, Gothic, Antique, and Dark Age asset packs included, with new theme forks shipping every quarter.

🚚Station-Stop Logistics

Order during your shift, collect on your way out. Timing-optimized virtual delivery routes purchases straight to your desk before you've clocked off — built by employees who actually use it.

🌍Void-Layer World Generation

New environments grow from foundation plates rather than being hand-built — raw concept, written as rules, takes root and a world forms around it. Adaptive, scalable, occasionally unsupervised.

🔒MonoSec Protection Suite

Integrated monitoring across every databoard, terminal, and instance. Threat detection, access control, and employee wellness tracking, unified under one security team.

🏢District Architecture

Modular city-scale campuses that scale horizontally as headcount grows. Add a District, connect it to the network, done — no downtime, no service interruption anyone will admit to.
