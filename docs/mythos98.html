Product

MythOS98 — The Operating System for Life Itself

One unified virtual experience for work, leisure, logistics, and everything in between. MythOS98 is Monogon's most complete release yet. Built to be the last thing you'll ever need.

Core Features

💼Simulated Workspaces

Full office environments rendered on demand. Work from home without ever leaving the office. Commute times drop to zero, load times become your new traffic.

👑FantasyLand Integration

Warp straight from your workspace into a fully themed leisure instance. Medieval, Gothic, Antique, and Dark Age asset packs included, with new theme forks shipping every quarter.

🚚Station-Stop Logistics

Order during your shift, collect on your way out. Timing-optimized virtual delivery routes purchases straight to your desk before you've clocked out. Built by the employees who actually use it.

🌍Void-Layer World Generation

New environments grown from foundational ideas rather than being hand-built. Raw concepts, taking root and the worlds form around it. As one of our lead engineers put it, it's bio digital jazz man.

🔒MonoSec Protection Suite

Integrated monitoring across every databoard, terminal, and instance. Threat detection, access control, and employee wellness tracking, unified under one security team. With correction and expungement by the cleanup crews.

🏢District Architecture

Modular districts that can scale vertically or horizontally as headcount grows. Add a District, connect it to the network, done — no downtime, no service interruptions.
