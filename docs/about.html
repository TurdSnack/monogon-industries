Company

About Monogon Industries

We don't like to think of ourselves as a technology company. We think of ourselves as the substrate the world runs on.

Monogon Industries designs, builds, and secures MythOS. The simulated environment that quietly underpins work, leisure, retail, and civic life across every District. From FantasyLand to the Museum of Technical Demonstration, if it feels real, there's a decent chance Monogon built the floor it's standing on.

Mission

"Reality is just the first draft."

We believe the world people actually want to live in doesn't have to be the one they were handed. MythOS lets us iterate on that. New Districts, new leisure instances, new ways to spend your lunch break. Faster than physical infrastructure ever could. Every fork, every FantasyLand theme, every Station-Stop route exists because someone at Monogon decided the current draft wasn't good enough yet.
